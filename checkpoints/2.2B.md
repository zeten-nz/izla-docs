## Revision transitions
- `POST /revisions/:id/submit-review` (content.author): DRAFT → REVIEW; review-readiness gate; `updatedBy` = actor; does
  NOT set reviewedBy/publishedBy.
- `POST /revisions/:id/return-to-draft` (content.publish): REVIEW → DRAFT; mandatory `reason` → `StaffAudit.reason`.
- `POST /revisions/:id/publish` (content.publish): REVIEW → PUBLISHED (below). No direct DRAFT → PUBLISHED. REVIEW stays
  frozen for authoring (2.2A DRAFT-only guards unchanged).
- `reviewedBy`/`publishedBy` are both stamped = publishing actor at publication (self-publish: createdBy=reviewedBy=publishedBy).

## Atomic publication (§17-22)
- **Lesson lock:** `SELECT id FROM "lesson" WHERE id = $1 FOR UPDATE` inside the tx serializes publish + takedown.
- **revision OCC:** `expectedRevisionUpdatedAt`; **Lesson OCC:** `expectedLessonUpdatedAt` (both required for an actual publication).
- **old revision:** if replacing, old current PUBLISHED → ARCHIVED (guarded on PUBLISHED + same Lesson; coherence-checked;
  history/publishedBy/publishedAt kept).
- **new revision:** PUBLISHED + reviewedBy + publishedBy + publishedAt + `estimatedDurationMin` (sum iff all present, else null).
- **pointer:** `Lesson.status = PUBLISHED`, `publishedRevisionId = revisionId`; both timestamps strictly advance. One
  transaction with StaffAudit — no visible intermediate state.
- **idempotent republish:** success no-op ONLY when the revision is the FULLY-coherent current publication — Lesson
  PUBLISHED **and** Revision PUBLISHED **and** `publishedRevisionId = revisionId` **and** `Revision.lessonId = Lesson.id`
  (no change, no 2nd audit, even with stale tokens). After an urgent takedown the pointer/revision are unchanged but
  `Lesson.status = ARCHIVED`, so this deliberately does NOT match: republish-after-takedown fails safe with a lifecycle
  conflict (checked BEFORE any OCC token comparison → no restore, no timestamp change, no audit, whatever tokens are sent).

## Pointer invariants
After publish: Lesson PUBLISHED, `publishedRevisionId = newRevision.id`, `newRevision.lessonId = Lesson.id`,
`newRevision.status = PUBLISHED`, and exactly ONE PUBLISHED revision for the Lesson. Not required to be the max version.

## Replacement history / takedown
- **Replacement:** old PUBLISHED → ARCHIVED, new current; a pinned learner keeps resuming the OLD pinned revision (never
  repinned); a NEW learner pins the new current revision.
- **Takedown** (`POST /lessons/:id/archive`, content.publish, mandatory reason): PUBLISHED Lesson → ARCHIVED. Removes
  ALL learner access WITHOUT moving the pointer, archiving the revision, or deleting progress/completion/attempt history.
- **Takedown vs replacement distinction (canonical `resumableLessonWhere`):** REVISION REPLACEMENT keeps the Lesson
  PUBLISHED → the old pinned learner may continue on the old revision; URGENT TAKEDOWN sets `Lesson.status = ARCHIVED` →
  the learner may NOT continue. Every learner execution surface reuses the ONE `resumableLessonWhere(...)` authority
  (Lesson + full Subject→Topic PUBLISHED; the pinned revision MAY be ARCHIVED; NOT required to equal the current
  `publishedRevisionId`) as its gate, evaluated BEFORE any idempotent replay / attempt create / step / signal / mission /
  completion / review work:
  - **execution:** `submitActivityAttempt` and resume → `LESSON_NOT_EXECUTABLE`.
  - **completion:** `markViewOnlyStep` and `completeLesson` → `LESSON_NOT_READY_FOR_COMPLETION` (before normal AND
    idempotent completion).
  - **review session:** `start`/resume → `REVIEW_CANDIDATE_NOT_AVAILABLE`; `getSession` → `REVIEW_SESSION_NOT_FOUND`
    (404-safe, does not reveal hidden content exists); `submitAttempt`/`complete` → `REVIEW_SESSION_ACTIVITY_NOT_AVAILABLE`.
    Sessions/attempts are never deleted, cancelled, or repinned.

revisionVersion/previousPublishedRevisionId/firstPublication; reason in `StaffAudit.reason`. Never payload/answerKey/storageKey.
