## Summary
Telegram Login is a real **OIDC** (auth-code + PKCE; `iss https://oauth.telegram.org`, `aud`=BotID, JWKS published;
scopes `openid`/`profile`/`phone`/`telegram:bot_access`; stable identity = `sub`; phone consent-gated; no UserInfo
endpoint). Mini App auth = HMAC-SHA-256(`initData`) or the newer Ed25519 `signature`; Telegram IDs must use a **safe
non-32-bit representation** (decimal `String` rec / `BIGINT`); digital goods inside Telegram **must** use **Stars
(`XTR`)**. Izlan is already identity-agnostic below the phone layer (sessions/JWT/profile key off `userId`; nothing
dereferences `User.phone`), so a generic `UserIdentity` + nullable phone (Option B) is the recommended, low-risk path;
`User.id` stays the stable principal (no migration to user-ids/roles/progress/XP/IZL/subscriptions). **Cross-surface
identity gap:** OIDC `sub` is NOT documented as equal to the Bot/Mini App numeric `user_id` — store both and verify the
mapping **before 2.2T-D** (a **technical verification gate**, not an owner decision). Two other caveats: the Mini App session **transport is VERIFY-LATER**
(the same-site cookie may need adaptation; converge onto Izlan's session lifecycle, decide the credential after real
environment testing — do not put a long-lived refresh token in JS/localStorage), and suspension does not currently
revoke sessions. **Phase 2.2A-D (content schema hardening) is independent of Telegram and may proceed. No Telegram code;
CLICK/Payme track stays PAUSED.**
